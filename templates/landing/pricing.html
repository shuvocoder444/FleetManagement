Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing and managing your Topd subscription.

How does subscription purchasing work?

Simply choose your preferred plan on this page, click "Buy Subscription", and you will see our bank transfer and mobile wallet instructions. Complete the transfer through your banking app and submit the transaction reference code. Our operations team verifies and activates your subscription within 15–30 minutes.

Can I pay in Kyrgyzstani Som (KGS) or USD?

Yes! Both currencies are fully supported. You can pay via Demir Bank in USD or converted KGS Som using the official daily rate.

What is the "Pay Per Check" plan?

The Pay Per Check plan is tailored for occasional rental agencies or individual owners who only need to verify a driver once or twice. Each check costs only $2.00 without requiring a monthly commitment.

How do I track my active subscriptions and payment history?

Once logged in, open the "My Subscription" page from your dashboard or profile dropdown. You will see live expiration dates, payment receipts, and approval statuses.
